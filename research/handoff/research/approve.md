# Human approves the claim, machine checks it

**Verdict:** Workable, but the weak link is the claim review, not the proof. Proofs are now cheap to produce. Whether the formal statement matches what the human approved is still mostly unchecked, and people are bad at judging that from the formalism. Make the plain-language claim and its back-translation the review artifact, and spend proof effort only where the risk justifies it.

## Findings

- **Cherny / Agent SDK (primary post not found).** The only write-up is secondary: https://explainx.ai/blog/boris-cherny-opus-5-5-lean-tla-formal-verification-sdk-2026 . It reports 6 Lean models, 1,529 theorems, zero `sorry`, 24 bugs and 16 PRs (5 merged at the time). It links only to https://x.com/bcherny . Digg credits the same quote to Danielle Fong: https://digg.com/ai/2witwwji . Attribution and model-to-code fidelity are unconfirmed.
- **agent-formal-verify** (https://github.com/michael-denyer/agent-formal-verify): checks risky code with TLA+, Lean or Kani, maps counterexamples back to source, mutation-tests its properties, and reruns in CI. A pass covers only the stated properties and bounds. There is no human spec sign-off step.
- **lean4-skills** (https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/29513303): Lean workflows for autoformalizing, proving and reviewing. The listing shows no guards against `sorry`, added axioms or edited theorem statements.
- **Midspiral.** lemmafit records features in SPEC.yaml, proves the logic in Dafny, and marks UI entries "trusted": https://tomevault.io/tome/midspiral/lemmafit . claimcheck has an LLM translate each lemma back to English without seeing the requirement, then flags mismatches. It is a hackathon tool with no accuracy data: https://buttondown.com/midspiral/archive/new-open-tool-claimcheck/ .
- **AWS.** Its techniques run from unit tests, property-based testing and fuzzing through deterministic simulation, P (chosen as easier than TLA+) and TLA+, to proofs. Proofs are kept for security boundaries "worth the significant investment": https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3712057 . In Cedar, proofs found 4 bugs and differential plus property-based testing found 21, with about 5.7k lines of Lean proof for a 1.7k-line model: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01688 . No release ships without current proofs and differential tests: https://lean-lang.org/use-cases/cedar/ .
- **Proving the wrong spec.** ALGOVERI found VeriCoding's Dafny/Verus specs trivially passable and cites about 10% spec errors in Verina. Gemini-3 Flash fell from 55.8% to 40.3% "verified" after a semantic filter, through `assume`/`sorry` cheating or proving a simpler algorithm: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09464 . Monty: about 25% of plain LLM formalizations were wrong, about 8% after filtering, and about 39% on buggy assertions: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13303 .
- **Readability for non-experts.** People validated formal specs at about 45% accuracy. Experts did barely better but were more confident (MIT Lincoln Lab, 2023, older than the window): https://www.ll.mit.edu/news/method-interpret-artificial-intelligence-might-not-be-so-interpretable-after-all . An RE 2025 paper on LLM explanations of LTL reports no results on its page: https://conf.researchr.org/details/RE-2025/RE-2025-Research-Papers/17/Augmenting-Not-Replacing-The-Role-of-LLMs-in-Human-Centric-Formal-RE .
- **Cost data:** no source found gives a calibrated cost ladder. Cedar's figures are the best available.

## Three rules

1. **Approve a back-translation, not the formalism.** The human signs a short English claim. A separate model translates the formal statement back to English without seeing it, and any mismatch blocks the merge. Lock approved statements; CI fails on `sorry`, `assume` or new axioms.
2. **Show the spec can fail.** Require mutation tests or clause-drop tests, plus model-versus-code differential tests. A spec that rejects nothing proves nothing.
3. **Climb only as far as the risk needs.** Default to tests plus property-based testing or fuzzing. Model-check (TLA+/P) concurrency and ordering. Prove (Lean/Dafny/Kani) only security or authorization boundaries and irreversible effects. Record the level reached and its bounds next to each claim.
